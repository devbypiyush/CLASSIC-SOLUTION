1) Soil Application: Before Plantation The first step is to cultivate sugarcane.Any farmer who grows sugarcane should be aware that the land needs to be well-drained in order to have good water retention and avoid water logging. Loamy soil is ideal for growing sugarcane. It will help it grow more quickly if the soil's PH level is about 6.5. The farmer usually amends the soil with cow manure, DAP, and different fertilizers. You can use Vestige's Agri Humic Granules, Agri Protek, and Agri 82 with this fertilizer. Agri-humic granules can be used to improve the soil's fertility before planting the sugarcane crop, ensuring that the crop's roots have access to all the vital nutrients they need to thrive. Dose: Per acre, you can use 5 kilograms. Additionally, you can use Vestige Agri Protek in the soil to protect plants from numerous diseases and boost crop yield. Dose: Use at a dose of 500 g per acre. Agri 82 will activate your crops, causing them to become more active. It aids in enhancing the soil's capacity to retain water. Use doses of 150–200 ml per acre. Mix these 3 Vestige Products with your fertilizers in the proper dose.


Step 2 : Seed treatment Let us now discuss the second phase, Seed treatment. Sets are pieces of material used to sow sugarcane crops. Before planting, we can treat the seeds with Agri Humic and Agri Protek. Agri-humic helps strengthen roots and accelerate sugarcane growth. Agri Protek will help avoid numerous fungal diseases in crops as well as boost immunity. 1 liter of water can be used to make a solution of agrihumic and agri protek. The dose of agrihumic is 5 mL per litre of water and that of agri protek is 5–10 g per liter of water.

Agri Humic: If spraying, use 30 mL per 15 liters of water, or 400–500 mL per acre if using irrigation. Agri-humic works to improve soil fertility and sugarcane growth. Agri Gold, If you want to spray, use 20g per 15 liters of water. A dose of 100g per acre can be applied using irrigation. AgriGold, in particular, protects against both biotic and abiotic stress. It also helps to increase the height and size of sugarcane. Agri-82 is a third product that can be utilized. If you wish to use it as a spray, the dose is 5–10 ml per 15 liters of water.

What is a face scrub?
A face scrub is an amazing skincare product that helps exfoliate the skin. Exfoliation is the process of removing dead skin cells from the surface of your skin using a granular substance or exfoliation tool. Face scrub contains coarse particles, helping you to exfoliate the skin. After you apply a face scrub, the particles rub against the skin, helping to remove all the dirt. From removing dead skin cells to reducing the chances of breakouts, the face scrub helps the skin in many different ways. It also makes your skin smooth and soft. A facial scrub uses small particles, beads, or substances to get rid of old skin cells and make way for new ones in a process known as exfoliation. Exfoliating helps sluggish skin turn over its top dermal layer more often, so you can address a multitude of problems, such as flakiness or excess sebum. Unclog pores from dirt, blackheads, dead skin, and other surface impurities so that new, healthy skin may flourish.
